Bingo Dabber privacy policy

Last updated: 11 October 2026

Bingo Dabber is a free app for marking paper bingo tickets. It shows ads from Google AdMob.

What the app itself collects

Nothing. Bingo Dabber has no accounts and keeps no data on any server.

  • Photos of your tickets are read on your phone using Google's on-device text recognition (ML Kit). A cropped picture of each ticket is kept on your phone so the app can show it when that card wins. It's deleted when you remove the card. Photos are never uploaded anywhere.
  • Your cards and called numbers are saved only on your phone so a game survives if you close the app. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes them.
  • Friend codes that remove ads are checked on your phone. Nothing is sent anywhere.

Ads

The app shows a banner ad provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google's ads software collects:

  • your device's IP address (which can indicate your general location)
  • your Android advertising ID and similar device identifiers
  • how you interact with ads and the app (for example, ad taps)
  • basic diagnostic information about app performance

This data goes to Google and is handled under Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy. You can learn how Google uses data from apps that use its services here: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

In the UK, the EU and some other places, the app asks for your consent before showing personalised ads. You can change your choice at any time with Privacy options at the bottom of the main screen. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's Settings > Privacy > Ads.

Permissions

  • Camera: the app opens your phone's camera app so you can photograph tickets. It only receives the photo you take.
  • Vibration: used to buzz when one of your cards wins.
  • Internet: used only to load ads.

Children

Bingo Dabber is meant for adults and isn't directed at children.
