Bingo Dabber

Play every ticket at once. Never miss a number.

Photograph your paper bingo tickets, type each number as it's called, and Bingo Dabber dabs every card for you. It tells you when you're 1 to go and buzzes the moment you win.

Get it on Google Play Free for Android. Coming soon to Google Play.

Scan whole strips

Take a photo of a strip of 6 and every card is read on your phone. Any misread number is flagged before you play.

Line, two lines, full house

Cards closest to winning rise to the top, with exactly the numbers you're waiting for.

Your ticket, on a win

When a card wins, its photo pops up so you can find the right paper ticket in a hurry.
